The Rule

A year is a leap year if it is divisible by 4, except if divisible by 100, unless it is also divisible by 400. This correction keeps the calendar aligned with Earth's tropical year.

year % 4 == 0 && (year % 100 != 0 || year % 400 == 0)

Why It Matters

The Gregorian reform of 1582 trimmed the Julian drift by omitting three leap years every 400 years. Without it, Easter would eventually drift into summer (Meeus, 1991).
